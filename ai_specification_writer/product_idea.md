# Product Idea: Carpool Connect

## Vision
Make daily commuting more affordable, convenient, and sustainable by helping people find and share rides with others traveling in the same direction.

## Users
- **Drivers:** Want to share fuel and parking costs, fill empty seats, and find trustworthy passengers.
- **Passengers:** Want affordable, reliable transportation, convenient pickup and drop-off points, and safe rides.
- **Organizations and Communities:** Want to reduce traffic congestion and parking demand while encouraging sustainable commuting.

## Key Features
- **Ride Matching:** Match drivers and passengers based on routes, destinations, departure times, and available seats.
- **Ride Scheduling:** Allow users to create one-time or recurring rides and reserve seats in advance.
- **In-App Messaging:** Let matched users coordinate pickup locations and timing.
- **Profiles and Reviews:** Display user profiles, ride history, and ratings.
- **Cost Sharing:** Estimate trip costs and help participants agree on a fair contribution.
- **Notifications:** Send updates about ride requests, confirmations, and cancellations.
- **Safety Tools:** Allow users to report concerns and share trip details.

### Differentiators
- **Commute-focused matching:** Prioritize regular work, school, and university routes.
- **Flexible recurring rides:** Support repeating weekly schedules.
- **Transparent cost estimates:** Show estimated contributions before booking.
- **Community-based trust:** Encourage verified profiles and user ratings.
- **Sustainability insights:** Estimate fuel and emissions saved through ride sharing.

### Intended Impact
Carpool Connect aims to reduce commuting costs, make better use of available vehicle seats, and help communities reduce traffic congestion and environmental impact.
