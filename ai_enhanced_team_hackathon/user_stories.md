
# User Stories – FridgeMind

## Overview

FridgeMind is an AI-powered food management application that helps users organize ingredients, discover recipes, and reduce food waste.

## User Stories

### US-01: Add Ingredients
**As a** user,  
**I want** to add ingredients to my virtual fridge,  
**so that** I can keep track of the food I have.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Users can enter an ingredient name.
- Users can specify its quantity.
- The ingredient appears in the ingredient list.

**Priority:** MVP

### US-02: View Available Ingredients
**As a** user,  
**I want** to view all my stored ingredients,  
**so that** I know what food is available.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- All saved ingredients are displayed.
- Ingredient names and quantities are visible.
- Users can easily find an ingredient.

**Priority:** MVP

### US-03: Remove Ingredients
**As a** user,  
**I want** to remove ingredients I have used or no longer have,  
**so that** my virtual fridge stays accurate.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Users can select an ingredient to remove.
- The ingredient disappears from the list.
- The updated list is displayed.

**Priority:** High

### US-04: Generate AI Recipes
**As a** user,  
**I want** AI to suggest recipes based on my available ingredients,  
**so that** I can prepare meals without buying unnecessary food.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Users can request recipe suggestions.
- AI considers available ingredients.
- The system displays recipe names and instructions.

**Priority:** MVP

### US-05: Track Expiration Dates
**As a** user,  
**I want** to save expiration dates for ingredients,  
**so that** I can identify food that needs to be used soon.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Users can enter an expiration date.
- The expiration date is saved with the ingredient.
- Users can view upcoming expiration dates.

**Priority:** MVP

### US-06: Receive Expiration Alerts
**As a** user,  
**I want** to see alerts about ingredients close to expiration,  
**so that** I can use them before they go bad.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The system identifies ingredients close to expiration.
- Expiring ingredients are clearly highlighted.
- Users can view which ingredients need attention.

**Priority:** High

### US-07: Prioritize Ingredients in Recipes
**As a** user,  
**I want** recipe suggestions to prioritize ingredients close to expiration,  
**so that** I can reduce food waste.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The system identifies ingredients close to expiration.
- AI considers these ingredients when generating recipes.
- Suggested recipes indicate which ingredients they help use.

**Priority:** High

### US-08: View Recipe Details
**As a** user,  
**I want** to view recipe ingredients and preparation instructions,  
**so that** I can easily prepare a suggested meal.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Each recipe displays its ingredients.
- Preparation steps are clearly listed.
- Users can view the complete recipe.

**Priority:** MVP

### US-09: Edit Ingredient Information
**As a** user,  
**I want** to update ingredient quantities and expiration dates,  
**so that** my inventory remains correct.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Users can select an existing ingredient.
- Users can update its quantity or expiration date.
- Changes are saved and displayed.

**Priority:** Medium

### US-10: View Food Waste Summary
**As a** user,  
**I want** to see a summary of ingredients used before expiration,  
**so that** I can understand my progress in reducing food waste.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- The system displays basic ingredient usage information.
- Users can see how many ingredients were used.
- The summary is easy to understand.

**Priority:** Low
