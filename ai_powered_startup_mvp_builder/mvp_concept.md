# MVP Concept - SmartAdvisor

## Problem Statement

Many people struggle to organize their daily tasks, manage requests, and find quick answers to everyday questions. Switching between different applications can waste time and make it difficult to stay organized.

SmartAdvisor aims to solve this problem by providing a simple AI-powered assistant that helps users manage tasks, submit requests, and get helpful answers in one place.

## Target Users

- Students who need help organizing assignments and deadlines.
- Professionals who want to manage daily tasks more efficiently.
- Individuals who need quick answers and recommendations.
- People looking for a simple productivity assistant.

## Core Features

1. **AI Chat Assistant**  
   Allows users to ask questions and receive helpful AI-generated responses.

2. **Request Submission**  
   Provides a form where users can submit requests and describe what kind of assistance they need.

3. **Task Organization**  
   Helps users organize their tasks and keep track of important activities.

4. **Personalized Recommendations**  
   Provides suggestions based on users' questions and requests.

5. **Tab-Based Navigation**  
   Allows users to switch easily between the Requests and Chat sections.

## Constraints

- The MVP will focus on essential features only.
- An internet connection is required for AI-powered responses.
- AI responses may not always be completely accurate.
- User authentication is not included in the initial version.
- Advanced analytics and integrations with external applications are outside the MVP scope.
- The application will focus on a simple, responsive web interface.
